This STANDARD is not met as evidenced by:

treatment program for 6 of 6 audit clients (#1, #2,

an IPP.

current program plans for any of the clients could
be located.

revealed he was admitted to the facility on
4/17/23. Additional review of the record did not
include Occupational Therapy, Physical Therapy,
Speech Language, Nutrition, Dental, Vision, and
