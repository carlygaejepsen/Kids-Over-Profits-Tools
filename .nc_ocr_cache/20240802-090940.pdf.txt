W 130 PROTECTION OF CLIENTS RIGHTS
CFR(s): 483.420(a)(7)

The facility must ensure the rights of all clients.
Therefore, the facility must ensure privacy during
treatment and care of personal needs.

This STANDARD is not met as evidenced by:
Based on observations, record review and
interview, the facility failed to ensure 1 of 4 audit
clients (#1) were afforded privacy during personal
care and toileting. The finding are:

During observations in the home on 7/24/24 at
6:30am, client #1 was in the hallway and kitchen
area with a night shirt above her waistline and her
pull up was visible. Staff A assisted her to her
bedroom. Further observation at 6:45am, client
#1 was told by staff A to go to the bathroom.
Client #1 was in the bathroom with her pants
down and sitting on the toilet with the bathroom
open.

Review on 7/24/24 of client #1's
Community/Home Life assessment (dated
10/28/23) revealed client #1 requires verbal cues
or closing doors to bedroom/bathroom before
dressing, undressing or toileting.

nterview on 7/24/24 with staff A confirmed client
#1 needed verbal cues to close the door when
using the bathroom. Staff A also confirmed client
#1 requires verbal cues to be fully dressed before
leaving privacy of bedroom/bathroom.
