V118 27G .0209 (C) Medication Requirements
President met with staff to review and discuss the agency's Medication
Requirements policy and procedure.

Staff reviewed a Medication Administration Record (MAR) ofall drugs administered
to each member.

President discussed the importance of ensuring MAR is kept current for each
member, and is filled out correctly as evidenced by ensuring that each MAR includes
member's name; name; strength; and quantity of the medication; instructions on
administering the medication; date and time the medication is administered; and
name or initials of the person administering the medication and lastly that
medications are administered on the written order of a physician.

President along with Qualified Professional (QP) to conduct a daily review of each
member's MAR to ensure that medications are being administered on the written
order of a physician, that member’s MAR has been completed correctly, with a focus
of ensuring that the name or initials of person administering the medication is
present, and are current.

Quality Management Team to perform a monthly audit of all current member MARs
to ensure compliance with Medication Requirements and guidelines,
