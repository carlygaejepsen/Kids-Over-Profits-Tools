27G .0207 Emergency Plans and Supplies

10A NCAC 27G .0207 EMERGENCY PLANS
AND SUPPLIES

(a) Each facility shall develop a written fire plan
and a disaster plan and shall make a copy of
these plans available

to the county emergency services agencies upon
request. The plans shall include evacuation
procedures and routes.

(b) The plans shall be made available to all staff
and evacuation procedures and routes shall be
posted in the

facility.

(c) Fire and disaster drills in a 24-hour facility
shall be held at least quarterly and shall be
repeated for each shift.

Drills shall be conducted under conditions that
simulate the facility's response to fire
emergencies.

(d) Each facility shall have a first aid kit
accessible for use.
