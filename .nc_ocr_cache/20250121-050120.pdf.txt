V 114| 27G .0207 Emergency Plans and Supplies Vv 114

10A NCAC 27G .0207 EMERGENCY PLANS
AND SUPPLIES

(a) Each facility shall develop a written fire plan
and a disaster plan and shall make a copy of
these plans available

to the county emergency services agencies upon
request. The plans shall include evacuation
procedures and routes.

(b) The plans shall be made available to all staff
and evacuation procedures and routes shall be
posted in the

facility.

(c) Fire and disaster drills in a 24-hour facility
shall be held at least quarterly and shall be
repeated for each shift.

Drills shall be conducted under conditions that
simulate the facility's response to fire
emergencies.

(d) Each facility shall have a first aid kit
accessible for use.

This Rule is not met as evidenced by:

Based on record reviews and interviews, the
facility failed to conduct fire and disaster drills
once per shift per quarter. The findings are:

The facility had 4 shifts: Monday through Friday,
7am to 3pm, 3pm to 11pm and 11pm to 7am and
weekends from 7am to 7pm and 7pm to 7am.

Reviews on 1/8/25 and 1/9/25 of the facility's fire
and disaster drills revealed:

-No documentation in January 2024 of fire or
disaster drills being conducted.

-No documentation in June 2024 of fire or
disaster drills being conducted.

-No documentation in August 2024 of fire or
disaster drills being conducted.

-No documentation in October 2024 of fire or
disaster drills being conducted.

-No documentation in November 2024 of fire or
disaster drills being conducted.

-No documentation in December 2024 of fire or
disaster drills being conducted.

Interview on 1/8/25 with client #3 revealed:
-Had not participated in any fire or disaster drills
since her admission in November 2024.

Interview on 1/9/25 with the Director revealed:
-Was unable to locate documentation for all the
fire and disaster drills conducted.

-Would ensure the facility conducted both fire and
disaster drills once per shift per quarter.

This deficiency constitutes a re-cited deficiency
and must be corrected within 30 days.
