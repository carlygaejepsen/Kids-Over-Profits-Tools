assures the implementation of G.S. 122C-59,
G.S. 122C-65, and G.S. 122C-66.

implement policy to assure that:

(1) all instances of alleged or suspected
abuse, neglect or exploitation of clients are
reported to the County Department of Social
Services as specified in G.S. 108A, Article 6 or
