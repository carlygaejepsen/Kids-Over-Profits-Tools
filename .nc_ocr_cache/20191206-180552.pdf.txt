A. Finding:
10A NCAC 27G .0203 OP Competency

Corrective Action Steps:

Brighter Dayz LLC will ensure that all clients who are being considered for admission into the Brighter
Dayz Level III residential treatment program will be subjected to screening. During this screening,
clients will be asked a series of questions, aimed at determining their suitability to the program. Brighter
Dayz LLC will then complete an Admission Assessment (on company letterhead) which will be stored in
the client file, and available for review. In the event that a client does not have a PCP, Brighter Dayz
LLC will draft a treatment plan which will also be housed within the client file. These documents will be
compiled and reviewed by the ED/QP prior to receiving an authorization from any MCO.

In addition, Brighter Dayz LLC will schedule a CFT for all clients within the 30 day time frame to ensure
safety and comply with the requirement. During these meetings the QP will document the progression or
regression of the aforementioned goals. Brighter Dayz LLC will discuss these observations during CFT
meetings, where all members of the treatment team will be present. ED/AP/QP/LPC will also provide
input and update the document every 30 days with the progress of the client. The PCP will be updated
and stored in the client file, and disseminated to any relevant parties as evidence.

Responsible Party:
Executive Director
Qualified Professional
Associate Professional

Evidence:
Assessment completed within 10 days before admission
Treatment Plan: Completed upon admission, and every 30 days thereafter

C. Finding:

10A NCAC 27G .0205 Assessment and Treatment/Habilitation or Service Plan

Corrective Action Steps:

Brighter Dayz LLC will ensure that all clients who are being considered for admission into the
Brighter Dayz Level III treatment program will be subjected to screening. During this screening
(either by Facetime or face to face), clients will be asked a series of questions, aimed at
determining their suitability to the program. Questions are designed to address needs/strengths,
diagnoses, educational and medical needs. The in house LPCS can/will also partake in the
meeting addressing the clinical concerns, and reviewing clinical documents provided. At the
conclusion, Brighter Dayz LLC team members will meet to determine the treatment plan and if
the client will be admitted. QP/ED will then complete an Admission Assessment (on company
letterhead) which will be stored in the client file, and available for review.

Brighter Dayz LLC will ensure that a PCP and/or treatment strategy is in place for each client
within our care. This document will be constructed upon admission into the group home and
reviewed by QP/ED. This document will be compiled and reviewed by the ED/QP prior to
receiving an authorization from any MCO. Brighter Dayz will conduct a review of the existing
client files in order to ensure that all clients have accurate PCP’s/treatment plans in place that is
consistent with the current level of care (Level III).

D. Finding:

10A NCAC 27G .1704 Minimum Staffing
Corrective Action Steps:

Brighter Dayz will require that the ED/AP/QP are at the group home every day until new direct
care staff are hired, and on-boarded and trained in order to ensure compliance with staffing
requirements. Brighter Dayz LLC will post an advertisement on Indeed.com for additional direct
care staff, and create a schedule that has a minimum of two staff that are assigned for each shift.
The direct care staff who are considered for employment will have relevant group home
experience, as well as an understanding of Brighter Day LLC procedures. Brighter Dayz LLC
will also work to ensure that a minimum of two staff is present at all times when dealing with
clients, to ensure that all clients are safe within the home. In the event of a call-out or no show.
Brighter Dayz LLC will have a repository of staff available for on call coverage.

Responsible Party:

Executive Director
Qualified Professional
Associate Professional

Monitoring: Monthly Schedule with two staff scheduled prepared by ED

F. Finding:
10A NCAC 27G .1708 Transfer/ Discharge

Corrective Action Steps:

Brighter Dayz LLC will ensure that no client will be transferred/discharged without giving
parent/legal guardian 30 days notice in writing. During this period, Brighter Dayz LLC will
assist in finding placement, and ensure that at a minimum a PCP, CCA/Addendum are up to
date, in order to aid in finding placement, and provide these documents to the required parties
within a reasonable timeframe.

ED will ensure that a 30 day written notice will be sent to parent/legal guardian notifying them
of a client discharge/transfer. The ED/QP/AP/LPCS will also ensure that the PCP,
CCA/Addendum are placed in the client file and document referrals made. This information will
also be relayed to the appropriate parties.

Responsible Party:
Executive Director
Qualified Professional
Licensed Professional

Evidence: E-mail with PCP, CCA/Addendum, e-mail correspondence with DSS/MCO,
dissemination of aforementioned documents to relevant parties.

G. Finding:
10A NCAC 27G .0604 Incident Reporting Requirements

Corrective Action Steps:

Brighter Dayz LLC will ensure that all Incident Reports, especially those involving members of law
enforcement responding, be documented within the IRIS system. Brighter Dayzz LLC will ensure that all
strategies/techniques entailed within the PCP are exhausted prior to reaching out to the police in an effort
to restore order and control. A senior administrator will be present daily which will help mitigate any
outbursts, AWOL attempts, improving the overall safety for our clients.

Brighter Dayz LLC conducted retraining on Incident reporting requirements via mandatory direct care
staff meeting held on October 25, 2019. Brighter Dayz LLC will ensure that all direct care staff and those
with any monitoring responsibility were required to participate. Oversight of this process will be that of
the Agency QP and will be monitored by the LPC on an ongoing basis.

Responsible Party:
Qualified Professional

LPCS

Evidence: QP submissions page in IRIS, IRIS submission documentation kept in group

10A NCAC 27E .0108 Training in Seclusion, Physical Restraint and Isolation

Corrective Action Steps:
Brighter Dayz LLC has retrained all Direct Care staff in the proper techniques to perform

physical restraints. All clients will be restrained according to NCI interventions/techniques.
Restraints are discouraged but if the client ends up on the floor at any point, Brighter Dayz LLC
staff must put a pillow under the client’s head to ensure safety. Brighter Dayz LLC endured that
the instructor discussed the importance of not using restrictive interventions for any retaliatory
means. Brighter Dayz LLC Executive Director relayed that staff who do not follow protocol will
be immediately terminated. Also, as a part of training each staff member demonstrated
proficiency in performing the proper technique to perform approved restraints. Also, ED/QP and
