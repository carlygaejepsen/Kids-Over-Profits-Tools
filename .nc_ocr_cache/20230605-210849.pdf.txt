Thank you for your recent visit to Pinnacle Therapeutic Services, We

below to rectify the violations that were noted.

A complaint survey was completed on April 19, 2023. The complaint
was substantiated (Intake #NC00200641). Deficiencies were cited
(with the following cross-referenced citations: VO00 Regarding the
complaint on April 19, 2023. The investigation found the accusations to
be false (Intake #NC00200641). Governing Body Policies (V105) 27G
.0201 (A) (1-7) PINNACLE THERAPEUTIC SERVICES will review the
existing policies and procedures and adhere to the comparison of the
Code of Federal Regulations and will revise as applicable. This process
will be assigned to the Program Director.

V107: In response to these violations, each person above the age of 16
in the Licensed facility will receive a background check th rough
PINNACLE THERAPEUTIC SERVICES that includes a review of the Health
Care Registry as well as a criminal background check using the previous
5 years of residency. Management will provide a copy of the
background checks to the Program Director within 10 days. Background
checks will be kept in the employment files of all staff, PINNACLE
THERAPEUTIC SERVICES staff will ensure all hired employee files are
current including background checks, health care registry checks,
annual training, and personnel information.

V108: In response to this violation, the Qualified Professional (QP)
assigned to the member's case will retrain all direct care staff in the
staff using client-specific material (i.e., Assessments, Individualized
Service Plans, Behavioral Support Plans, etc.). When new direct care
staff are hired, the QP assigned to the member's case will provide
client-specific training prior to the staff working with the member. QP
will ensure all staff training is up to date and rescheduled prior to the
expiration date. The QP will complete the Client Specific Training Form
to document the training as well as record all the staff's training
completed on the Orientation Checklist. These forms will be kept in the
staff's employment file with management. Copies of the staff for
Pinnacle Therapeutic Services will be sent to the Program Director
within two weeks of completion. QP will continue ongoing monitoring
to ensure training has not expired.

V109: In response to this violation, the QP will complete monthly
monitoring of services in the facility. During that monitoring session,
the Health and Safety Checklist will be completed. The QP and Program
Director will be notified of any changes or updates to the facility. The
assigned QP will complete an immediate health and safety check-up for
Pinnacle Therapeutic Services. The QP will continue to complete

quarterly health and safety check-ups each quarter for all facilities. The
MCO completes an annual review for each facilities and provide its
findings to the Program Director, Owner, and assigned QP. The QP will
ensure each staff in the Home has an active and valid Supervision Plan
with scheduled monthly supervision sessions being recorded on the
Supervision Tracking Form. Health and Safety Checklist completed by
QPs (monthly) and LCSW (quarterly) are to be turned in to Program
Director by the 10th day of the following month. Any issues or feedback
will be reviewed with the Program Director and assigned QP to address
any concerns assigned to the Staff.

V111: In response to this violation, the QP will ensure notebooks
containing member-specific information and documentation (i.e.,
Assessment, Individual Service Plans, Treatment Goals, Task Analysis,
Behavior Plans, Medications/Orders, Communication Logs, etc.) will be
reviewed, and kept with the assigned staff member within the facility.
Notebooks containing member-specific information and documentation
will be developed and reviewed with assigned staff immediately. QPs
will provide evidence of notebooks and their contents to the Program
Director by the end of the week following implementation and review
with staff. The development/review of a notebook containing member-
specific information and documents is considered part of the initial
placement process into a facility. QP will update information as
required and train staff as it relates to any changes and updates.

V112: In response to this violation, the home staff will have access and
training to implement/use information from member-

specific documentation to support the member in reaching his/her
desired outcomes by QP immediately. Staff will learn and
implement/use information from member-specific documentation to

support the member in reaching his/her desired outcomes once
notebooks have been developed and reviewed. QPs will document staff
progress and report concerns or additional needs to the Program
Director as soon as possible. QPs will monitor the communication
log/sheet used by staff during monthly monitoring sessions. The
Notebook and its contents should be considered part of member-
specific training for new staff and documented accordingly on the
Orientation Checklist. QPs will use monthly supervision/monitoring to
assess the staff's competence and performance in supporting the
member with meeting expected outcomes. Staff will record information
and updates/changes on communication pages of members’ notebooks
on a weekly basis or as frequently as changes occur. V113 A. To ensure
immediate compliance on 05/18/2023, immediate ramifications were
provided by the Program Director (PD) to Certified Clinical Hemodialysis
Technician (CCHT) #1 who failed to perform hand hygiene and don of
new gloves according to policy. The policies reviewed were Hand
Hygiene (Attachment A). Special emphasis was placed on performing
hand hygiene and donning new gloves before vascular access, after
touching inanimate objects, and before direct patient contact. B. All
direct patient care (DPC) staff will be re-in serviced by the Qualified
Professional (QP) on the Hand Hygiene Policy (Attachment A), and
Personal Protective Equipment Policy (Attachment B). Focus on proper
hand hygiene and glove use will be reinforced at the meeting. The in-
services will be completed by 05/18/2023. Documentation of the in-
servicing is attached (Attachment A1 & B1) along with the current DPC
roster (Attachment C). For ongoing compliance, the House Staff will
conduct daily audits of all DPC staff on each shift providing care to the
patients. Unannounced hand hygiene and personal protective
equipment use audits using a Plan of Correction (POC) monitoring tool
(Attachment D) will be performed daily for 1 week on all shifts until

100% compliance is achieved for 3 consecutive days. Monitoring will
then be decreased as follows: Frequency will be decreased to 3 times a
week, all shifts. When 100% compliance is achieved for 3 consecutive
evaluations of all shift’s tri-weekly, the frequency will be decreased to
weekly, all shifts. When 100% compliance is achieved for 3 consecutive
weeks, the frequency will be decreased to monthly, all shifts. If 100%
compliance is achieved for all shifts at monthly observation the auditing
will be completed monthly following the Quality Assessment
Performance Improvement (QAPI) audit calendar (Attachment E)
utilizing the completed POC Audit Tool (Attachment S) All audit findings
will be reviewed by the QP monthly during QAPI meetings. Sustained
compliance will be monitored by the QP/Program Director. Non-
adherence by the staff will result in re-education.

V115: In response to this violation, the home staff will be directed and
trained to maintain supervision at all times of members assigned to
their care. QP will review supervision and job description with all
assigned staff. Members are not to be left in the care or supervision of
non-staff at any time. The QP will complete Space and Supervision
training as part of the Provider Responsibilities Acknowledgement Form.
QP will notify the Program Director within two weeks of completion
and keep a copy of the Acknowledgement in the employment file with
Management.

V118: In response to this violation, the home staff will complete
Medication Administration training with QP (TBD). During the training,
staff will learn to appropriately complete a MAR (Medication
Administration Record) as well as identify the necessity of a service
order. SCS Licensed professional will provide QP with a copy of the BSP
approved by the Human Rights Committee for a doctor's signature

(service order) to be secured for any restrictive interventions required.
All Home staff will have "Certificates" of training or re-training in Med
Administration from QP as well as updated MAR with required service
orders under double lock in Home. Training records will be submitted
to the Program Director for review and placed in the employment file
with Management. Copies of service orders will be placed in a
notebook for the QP and Program Director to review within 2 weeks of
completion. A copy of BSP with the Physician’s signature will be placed
in a notebook as well within two weeks. V123 27G .0209 (H)
Medication Requirements (H) Medication errors. Drug administration
errors and significant adverse drug reactions shall be reported
immediately to a physician or pharmacist. An entry of the drug
administered, and the drug reaction shall be properly recorded in the
drug record. A client's refusal of a drug shall be charted.
