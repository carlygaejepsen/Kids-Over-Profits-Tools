Response to Statement of Deficiencies:

V109: 27G .0203 Competencies of Qualified Professionals and Associate
Professionals:

Executive Leadership Team met to review and discuss agency’s policies and
procedures regarding its New Hire Orientation Process and internal ongoing
training and supervision program. Qualified Professional (QP) and Associate
Professional (AP) was provided refresher training on population served, which
included a detailed review of each member’s Person Centered Plans (PCP), Crisis
Intervention Plan, and Incident Reporting training. Quality Management (QM) Team
will continue to identify trainings held by Managed Care Organizations (MCOs) and
other providers in efforts of ensuring that staff is provided with training
opportunities that will allow for the demonstration knowledge, skills and abilities
required by the population served. QM Team will conduct an audit of all HR records
on a monthly basis to ensure that monthly supervision is being provided to all
associate professionals by a qualified professional with the child/adolescent
population for the period of time as specified in Rule .0104. QM Team to perform
monthly review of supervision plans to ensure that staff continues to demonstrate
knowledge, skills and abilities required by the population served. QM Team to
conduct quarterly reviews of all staff charts to ensure that all staff has actively
participated and completed necessary core trainings at time of hire, annually
thereafter and as needed. Staff training and supervision log will be maintained in all
staff charts as evidence of competencies of all staff. Completion Date:

12/31/2024. Ongoing.

G.S. 131E-256 (G) HCPR- Notification, Allegations, & Protection:

Quality Management (QM) Team met to discuss the agency’s process for reporting
all allegations against health care personnel, including injuries of unknown source
to the Department. Agency’s policy was modified to include that all allegations
against health care personnel, including injuries of unknown source, be reported to
the Department, further that all alleged acts are investigated and results of the
investigation are reported to the Department within five working days of the initial
notification to the Department. Completion Date: 12/31/2024. Ongoing.

10A NCAC 27G .0603 Incident Reporting Requirements (V366)

QM Team conducted an internal Incident Reporting training with all staff. Training
consisted of a review of agency’s policy and procedures for incident reporting and
response to Level I, II & II incidents. Training also consisted of how to complete

agency’s internal incident reporting form, process and timeframes to be followed
when reporting a Level I II or III incident. Agency's Client Rights Committee (CRC) to
review all incidents reported on a quarterly basis, and as needed to ensure that
process is followed regarding reporting a Level I I] or II] incident. QM Team to
review all incidents to ensure incident has been reported and documented
according to reporting guidelines. QM Team to ensure during a monthly review that
copy of IRIS report and all correspondences/follow ups regarding incident is filed in
agency’s Incident Reporting binder. Completion Date: 12/31/2024. Ongoing.

V367: 10A NCAC 27G .0604 Incident Reporting Requirements for Category A
and B Providers:

QM Team held a meeting with staff to review and discuss the agency’s policy and
procedure for reporting incidents. Incident Reporting training was held with all staff
and included a review of agency’s process on how to document incidents on
agency’s internal incident reporting form, as well as entering Level I] and Level III
incidents into IRIS and required reporting timeframes. Agency’s CRC will monitor
the compliance of reporting a Level II and III incident to the MCO responsible for the
catchment area where services were provided within 72 hours of becoming aware
of the incident. QM Team will monitor all Level I] incidents to ensure conformance
to Level II incident reporting. Completion Date: 12/31/2024 Ongoing.

V 736 27G .0303(c) Facility and Grounds Maintenance 10A NCAC 27G .0303(c)
Location and Exterior Requirements:

Owner has made contact with several contractors to assist with ensuring that the
agency is in compliance with identified areas of concerns regarding safety. Owner
held meeting with staff to discuss how to maintain and ensure the cleanness, orderly
and attractive manner of the agency. Safety Committee to perform monthly internal
inspections to ensure that agency is in compliance with facility, grounds, location
and exterior requirements. Completion Date: 12/31/2024. Ongoing.
