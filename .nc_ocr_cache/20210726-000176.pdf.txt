(a) Medication dispensing:

(1) Medications shall be dispensed only on the

licensed to prescribe.

dispensing.

Finding #2:

Review on 5/26/21 of client #2's record revealed:
-13 year-old male admitted 1/30/21.

-Diagnoses included ADHD, Conduct Disorder,
and Disinhibited Social Engagement Disorder.

Review on 5/25/21 and 5/26/21 of client #2's
orders and MARs revealed:

-Guanfacine 1 mg, ordered 5/14/20 to be taken
twice daily, was scheduled to be administered at
7 am and 7 pm (ADHD).

-Depakote ER (extended release) 500 mg,
ordered 2/12/20 to be taken twice daily, was
scheduled to be administered at 7 am and 7 pm
(mood).

-Geodon 60 mg, ordered 2/12/20 to be taken

Observations on 5/26/21 at approximately
4:30pm revealed:

-Plastic cup with initials of client #1 written on a
piece of tape adhered to the side. Inside the cup
were 3 white tablets.

-Plastic cup with initials of client #2 written on a
piece of tape adhered to the side.

-Medications were counted at the beginning of
each shift.

-As she counted the medications she would pour
any client medications to be given during her shift
into the cup with that client's initials.

-The medications inside the cup labeled with
client #1's initials were his 7 pm medications,
Lamotrigine 25mg, Quetiapine 50mg, and
Buspirone 5 mg.

-She had completed a medication class prior to
administering medications. This pre-pouring
process was not taught during the class.
