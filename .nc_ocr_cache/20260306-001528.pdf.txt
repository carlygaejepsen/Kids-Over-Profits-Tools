Corrective Action Taken:

The staffing issue was immediately corrected on 02/04/2026 when the delayed staff
member reported to the residential facility, restoring compliance with the required staffing
ratio. No members were left without supervision, and no further instances of

Systemic Changes to Prevent Recurrence:

The residential facility has implemented a formal backup staffing procedure requiring the
availability of on-call staff for each shift. Staff is required to immediately notify the
Executive Director if a scheduled staff member is delayed or unable to report as scheduled.
Staffing verification is now completed prior to assuming responsibility for member
supervision.

Monitoring and Compliance:

The Qualified Professional will verify staffing ratios at the start of each shift and document
compliance on daily staffing logs. The Qualified Professional will review staffing
documentation weekly for 90 days and monthly thereafter to ensure ongoing compliance.

Responsible Persons:
