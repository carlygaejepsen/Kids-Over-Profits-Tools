Policies:

The current policy that Silver Ridge follows is
based on CDC guidelines healthcare/ behavioral
health facilities: “Patient Placement: For
patients with COVID-19 or other respiratory
infections, evaluate need for hospitalization, If
hospitalization is not medically necessary, home
care is preferable if the individual’s situation
allows.” CDC guidelines also state, “Telehealth
services should be optimized, when available
and appropriate. Consider whether your facility
can provide care in the safest way possible,
including optimizing telehealth services, when
available and appropriate.”

Because Silver Ridge is not a medical facility or
inpatient psychiatric facility, the program has the
lability, as recommended by the CDC, to provide
elehealth services for clients that are able to care
for themselves at home without the risk of
exposing other clients to infection.

Clients at Silver Ridge are discharged home, if
safe to do so, and are able to return to the facility
once they are symptom free and have completed
@ quarantine as designated by a medical
rofessional. In this instance, clients were
provided with the option to transition from
supervised living to day treatment at a hotel and
remain in clinical services. All of the clients in
question opted to do so and were not reporting
lany imminent psychiatric or medical risk at time
of transition. An immediate evaluation of client
condition is sometimes needed when there is a
change in family situation, medical issue,
occupational issue, etc.
