New Vision Home — MHL #060-852 — Plan of Correction

Introduction and Governance Response

Dreams and Vision, LLC takes the findings of the April 24, 2026 complaint and follow-up survey
with utmost seriousness. The Type A1 rule violation cited under 10A NCAC 27G .1701 (V293),
and its cross-referenced tags, reflect systemic gaps in clinical decision-making, supervisory
oversight, staffing, treatment planning, and admissions appropriateness that placed clients and
staff at risk of harm. The Executive Director / Licensee acknowledges personal responsibility for
the conditions identified, including the boundary-crossing decision to transport a client to her
personal residence, the failure to advocate for transition to a higher level of care when staff and
clinical partners had repeatedly recommended it, and the failure to maintain consistent
enforcement of program rules and consequences.

This Plan of Correction outlines specific, measurable, and time-bound actions that the facility
will implement to bring each cited tag into compliance. The corrective measures are organized
by tag, with each tag including: (1) the specific corrective action(s), (2) measures to prevent
recurrence, (3) the individual responsible for monitoring, and (4) the frequency of monitoring.

Note on existing Plans of Protection: The four Plans of Protection submitted between April
23-24, 2026 (POP, POP #1, POP #2, POP #3, POP #4) are superseded by this Plan of
Correction. Where prior actions remain valid (e.g., hiring of an additional Associate Professional
on April 13, 2026), they are referenced and built upon below. Where prior plans were incomplete
or untested, this document substitutes more rigorous measures.
