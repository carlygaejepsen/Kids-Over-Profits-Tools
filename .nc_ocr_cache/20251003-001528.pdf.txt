Division of Health Service Regulation

Community Treatment Alternatives II (4901 Rosena Drive, Charlotte, NC 28227)
Date Survey Completed: September 11, 2025

Response to Statement of Deficiencies:

10A NCAC 27G .0304 Facility Design and Equipment (V752)

Upon notification, Qualified Professional/Owner/ Director immediately tested the
water temperatures at all fixtures accessible to residents. Adjustments were made
to the water heater settings to ensure compliance. As of September 11, 2025, all hot
water temperatures are within the required range of 100 to 116 degrees Fahrenheit.
A licensed plumber was consulted to assess and recalibrate the water heater and
mixing valves to ensure consistent and safe water temperatures. Staff will conduct
weekly hot water temperature checks using a calibrated thermometer. A
temperature log has been implemented and will be reviewed monthly by the
Qualified Professional/Owner/Director. All residential counselors were provided
with refresher training on safe water temperature standards and how to document
and report irregularities. Ongoing training on environmental safety will be
conducted as needed and continued to be included in new hire orientation. The
Qualified Professional/Owner/Director will be responsible for ensuring continued
compliance with water temperature regulations and monitoring logs.

Date completed: 9/22/2025. Ongoing.
