27D .0103 Client Rights - Search And Seizure
Policy

10A NCAC 27D .0103 SEARCH AND
SEIZURE POLICY

(a) Each client shall be free from unwarranted
invasion of privacy.

(b) The governing body shall develop and
implement policy that specifies the conditions
under which searches of the client or his living
area may occur, and if permitted, the procedures
for seizure of the client's belongings, or property
in the possession of the client.

(c) Every search or seizure shall be documented.
Documentation shall include:

(1) scope of search;

(2) reason for search;

(3) procedures followed in the search;

(4) a description of any property seized;
and

(5) an account of the disposition of seized
property.
