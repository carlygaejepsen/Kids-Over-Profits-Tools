V 121} 27G .0209 (F) Medication Requirements v 121

10A NCAC 27G .0209 MEDICATION
REQUIREMENTS

(f) Medication review:

(1) If the client receives psychotropic drugs, the
governing body or operator shall be responsible
for obtaining a review of each client's drug
regimen at least every six months. The review
shall be to be performed by a pharmacist or
physician. The on-site manager shall assure that
the client's physician is informed of the results of
the review when medical intervention is indicated.

be recorded in the client record along with
corrective action, if applicable.
