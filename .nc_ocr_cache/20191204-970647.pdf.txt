Review on 11/26/19 of a "Bed Bug Procedures"
revealed the following documented:

"4. Report of Bedbugs is submitted through work
request system;

2. Member of maintenance staff will do visual
check of room the same day;

3. If no evidence is found room will be sprayed
along cracks and crevices;

4. If evidence is found room will be sprayed.
Depending on amount of infestation, mattresses
will be either heat treated or discarded. Furniture
will be treated or discarded as well.

5. Linens and clothing will be laundered and heat
treated in dryers;

6. Additional items will be heat treated;

7. Depending on severity of infestation, room
residents will be relocated to another room only
taking items that have been treated;

8. Infested room will remain vacant and will be
treated in 7 days;

9. Room will be inspected again after second
spraying. If no evidence is found, room will be
monitored until it is needed. if evidence is still
fond, room will continue to be retreated.

10. Adjacent room will be inspected and sprayed
along cracks and crevices;

11. Room treatment will be logged."
