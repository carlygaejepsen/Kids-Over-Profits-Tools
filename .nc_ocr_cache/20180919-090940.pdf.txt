W 368 | DRUG ADMINISTRATION
CFR(s): 483.460(k)(1)

The system for drug administration must assure
that all drugs are administered in compliance with
the physician's orders.

This STANDARD is not met as evidenced by:
Based on record review and interview, the
facility's drug administration system failed to
assure all drugs were administered in compliance
with physician's orders for 1 of 1 audit clients
(#6). The finding is:

Client #6 did not receive her prescribed
Benztropine 1mg due to unavailability prior to
9/4/18.

During observations of the medication pass on
9/5/18 at 5:39am, client #6 received Benztropine
0.5mg along with her other prescribed
medications. When the medications were being
checked for accuracy, it was noted client #6's
current physician's order for Benztropine
Mesylate is 1 MG Oral Tablet. Client #6's
Benztropine Mesylate "1 MG" medication was
later located on the very top shelf of the
medication cabinet and not filed in client #6
medication bin. The medication was delivered to
the facility on 9/4/18 (in the presence of the
surveyor). However, client #6's old medication
Benztropine 0.5mg medication remained in her
medication bin and was not removed nor
replaced with the new Benztropine dosage of
1mg (received at the facility on 9/4/18).

dated 8/2018 revealed, "Benztropine Mesylate 1
