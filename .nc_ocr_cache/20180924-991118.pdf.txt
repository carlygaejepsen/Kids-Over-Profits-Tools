Residential Treatment
Fire and Disaster Drill Procedures

Residential Cottage Supervisors complete a review of the Agency Disaster
Plan with each new student admitted to the Cottage within 24 hours.

Residential Programs conduct two Emergency Disaster Drills each month:
e Fire Drill
e Disaster Drill (One disaster drill needs to be done each month; Disaster drills will be initiated

by the Facilities Supervisor. Program staff are required to document the drill and indicate on Drill
Log the type conducted)

o Tornado Drill
Earthquake Drill
Chemical/Hazardous Materials Release

fe)

Evacuation due to a Bomb Threat
Secure Campus Lockdown (Partial and Full)

oo

Monthly Drills will be conducted quarterly on each shift per the schedule
below. Drills are to be conducted under conditions which simulate the
emergency.

Drills may be conducted on the same day provided it is clear they are separate
drills and documented as such, however, it is preferable to do them on
different days when possible.

Drills must be completed by the 15" of each month. Cottage supervisors are
required to scan each cottage’s drill log and email to the Residential
Administrative Assistant by the 25" each month.

The Residential Administrative Assistant will notify the respective Cottage
Supervisor, Residential Director and PQI team of any incomplete drills on the
26" each month, or the first business day following the 25".

The Residential Administrative Assistant will send an email confirmation that
all monthly drills have been completed by the 1* of the month (for the month
prior) to the Residential Director and PQI
