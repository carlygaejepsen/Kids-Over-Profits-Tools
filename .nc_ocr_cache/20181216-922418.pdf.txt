Complaint Survey 11/1/18

W 149 STAFF TREATMENT OF CLIENTS

The facility must develop and implement written policies that prohibit mistreatment, neglect or
abuse of the client.

Immediately upon exit of the surveyors on 11/1/18, staff were instructed and trained to increase
supervision of Client #16 until the silent alarm system called for in Client #16’s behavior support
plan is implemented. An additional staff member was instructed to remain in Hawksbill each 3
shift, pending the alarm installation, and instructed to complete 15 minutes bedchecks.

The silent alarm was installed on 11/13/18, and staff were trained on its use. Nightly, the alarm
will be tested prior to its use, in order to ensure its effectiveness in preventing the mistreatment,
neglect or abuse of clients who reside in Hawksbill group home.

Regular clinical assessment and any follow-up thereby identified will be conducted by the clinical
team, in order to ensure continued compliance with the expectation that the facility must
develop and implement written policies that prohibit mistreatment, neglect or abuse of the
client.
