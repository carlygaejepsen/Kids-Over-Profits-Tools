V 114) 27G .0207 Emergency Plans and Supplies

10A NCAC 27G .0207 EMERGENCY PLANS
AND SUPPLIES

(a) Each facility shall develop a written fire plan
and a disaster plan and shall make a copy of
these plans available

to the county emergency services agencies upon
request. The plans shall include evacuation
procedures and routes.

and evacuation procedures and routes shall be
posted in the

repeated for each shift.

V 736] 27G .0303(c) Facility and Grounds Maintenance V 736

odor.

the facility revealed:

counter had:

brown and 3 orange-colored spots.

brown-colored participles on the base from the
front of the turntable to the back edges and on
the left and right sides. A brown and white
substance approximately quarter-sized was
observed on the left side of the turntable.
