INITIAL COMMENTS

An annual and follow up survey was completed
on February 7, 2025. A deficiency was cited.

This facility is licensed for the following service
category: 10A NCAC 27G .1900 Psychiatric
Residential Treatment for Children and
Adolescents.

This facility is licensed for 12 and has a current
census of 9. The survey sample consisted of
audits of 3 current clients.

27G .0303(c) Facility and Grounds Maintenance

10A NCAC 27G .0303 LOCATION AND
