Below please find response detail to the standard level deficiencies identified during the
annual survey completed on June 27", 2025.

¢ 10A NCAC 27G .0209 MEDICATION REQUIREMENTS - ID Prefix Tag V118
Finding Summary - This rule is not met as evidenced by: Based on record reviews
and interviews, the facility failed to ensure medication were administered on the
written order of a physician affecting 1 of 3 audited clients.

Facility Response to Finding:

Future Leaders acknowledge the findings regarding 10A NCAC 27G .0209
Medication Requirements. Future Leaders have taken the following steps to
address this finding. Future Leaders will add an additional process step in the MAR
review process performed by AP or House Manager. The process step will include
providing notification to physicians of any consumer with medication coming within
a 2-week supply of needing a refill. Notification will be sent to the physician via my
chart, or email to evidence communication. If no physician acknowledgment of
notification is received within two days, an escalation will be provided to ED and QP
for assistance. Monthly report of consumer medication supply will be provided to
ED for awareness.

e G.S. 131 E-256 HEALTH CARE PERSONNEL REGISTRY - ID Prefix Tag V131
Finding Summary —This rule is not met as evidenced by: Based on record reviews,
the facility failed to access the Health Care Personnel Registry (HCPR) prior to
making an offer of employment.

Facility Response to Finding: RECEIVED
JUL 16 2025

DHSR-MH Licensure Sect

Future Leaders acknowledge the findings regarding G.S. 131 E-256 Health Care
Personnel Registry. Future Leaders have taken the following steps to address this
finding. As part of the new hire process a task will be included to ensure that the
Health Care Personnel Registry check has been completed. Process must be
completed before an offer for employment is made to a candidate. To ensure this
process is not skipped a new hire checklist will be created including this process
check. The new hire checklist will be reviewed and signed off by ED and QP
validating that all required steps have been completed for new candidate hire.
Offers for employment will not be provided prior to ED and QP review of the New
Hire Checklist. The checklist will be saved in the employee file.

G.S. 122C-80 CRIMINAL HISTORY RECORD CHECK - ID Prefix Tag V133

Finding Summary — This rule is not met as evidenced by: Based on record reviews
and interviews the facility failed to request a criminal history record check within 5
days of making a conditional offer of employment.

Facility Response to Finding:

Future Leaders acknowledge the findings regarding G.S. 122C-80 Criminal History
Record Check. Future Leaders have taken the following steps to address this
finding. As part of the new hire process a task will be included to ensure that the
Criminal History Record Check has been completed. Process must be completed
before an offer for employment is made to a candidate. To ensure this process is
not skipped a new hire checklist will be created including this process check. The
new hire checklist will be reviewed and signed off by ED and QP validating that all
required steps have been completed for new candidate hire. Offers for employment
will not be provided prior to ED and QP review of the New Hire Checklist. The
checklist will be saved in the employee file.
