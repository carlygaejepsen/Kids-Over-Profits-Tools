W 382 | DRUG STORAGE AND RECORDKEEPING
CFR(s): 483.460(1)(2)

The facility must keep all drugs and biologicals
locked except when being prepared for
administration.

This STANDARD is not met as evidenced by:
Based on observation and interviews, the facility
failed to assure all medications and biologicals
remained locked except when being prepared for
medication administration affecting 5 of 5 clients
(#1, #2, #3, #4, and #5). The finding is:

Observations on 2/18/26 at 6:30AM revealed both
doors to the medication room to be open. Further
observations revealed the medication cart to sit in
the medication room unlocked. Continued
observation at 6:50AM revealed staff H to
transition client #2 to the medication room.
Subsequent observation at 7:30AM revealed the
medication cart to remain open after medication
administration of several clients.

Review of the facility medication procurement and
storage policy on 2/18/26 indicated that
medications are stored in a locked medication
cart at all times. Review of the medication training
dated 1/22/26 revealed "when walking away from
the medication cart/closet, always lock the
cart/closet".

Interview with the facility nurse and qualified
intellectual disabilities professional (QIDP) on
2/18/26 verified that the medication cart should
not be left unattended and unlocked when it is not
being used. Further interview with the facility
nurse verified staff received a medication
administration refresher on 1/22/26 and have
been trained to keep the medication cart locked
when medications are not being administered to
