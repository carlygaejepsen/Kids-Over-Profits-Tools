(c) Each facility and its grounds shall be

This Rule is not met as evidenced by:

and its grounds were not maintained in a clean,
safe and orderly manner. The findings are:

revealed:

-A fist sized hole behind the door in the room
used by Former Client (FC) #3.

-Two switch plates were missing leaving exposed
wires in Client #2's room.

-The door leading from Client #2's room into the
bathroom had a fist sized hole in the middle of the
door above the doorknob, a hole approximately
10 inches wide by 5 inches tall at the bottom of
the door, and writing on the top half of the door.
