If a client is to be either transferred or discharged,
the facility must have documentation in the
client's record that the client was transferred or
discharged for good cause.

This STANDARD is not met as evidenced by:

interview, the interdisciplinary team failed to
demonstrate good cause for the discharge 1 of 1
client (#6) from the facility. The finding is:

non-compliance, disruptive behavior (i.e. yelling,

crying, kicking, screaming at the top of his voice,
cursing, throwing a tantrum, not able to control
his anger or emotions, etc.)

facility concerns with the recent change in
