Response to Summary Statement of Deficiencies

V 114 Our internal Safety Committee met and performed an audit of all fire and disaster
drills that were completed quarterly on each shift for the time period June 1, 2022
through March 13, 2023. Safety Committee reviewed all fire and disaster drills to ensure
that forms were filled out correctly, and fire and disaster drills were completed on each
shift. Binder that houses fire and disaster drills were reviewed to ensure that binder is
current with all completed fire and disaster drills. Staff meeting was held with all
residential staff. Staff meeting consisted of a review of agency’s policy regarding fire and
disaster drills and how to accurately complete all sections on agency’s fire and disaster
drill form. Staff was also reminded of the importance of conducting a debriefing session
with members after a fire and disaster drill so that members are able to communicate
the type of drill performed, how often and what times.
