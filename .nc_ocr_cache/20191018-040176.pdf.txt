INITIAL COMMENTS

Acomplaint and limited follow up survey for the
Type A1 rule violation was completed on
10/11/2019. This was a limited follow up survey,
only 10A NCAC 27G .0203 Competencies of
Qualified Professionals and Associate
Professionals (V109) and 10A NCAC 27G .1704
Minimum Staffing Requirements (V296) were

unsubstantiated (intake #NC155691). A
deficiency was cited.
