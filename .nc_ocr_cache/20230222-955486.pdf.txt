W 156 STAFF TREATMENT OF CLIENTS W 156
CFR(s): 483.420(d)(4)

The results of all investigations must be reported
to the administrator or designated representative
or to other officials in accordance with State law
within five working days of the incident.

This STANDARD is not met as evidenced by:
Based on record review and staff interviews, the
facility failed to report the required initial and five
days working investigation reports to mandatory
state agencies and law enforcement, for an
allegation of physical abuse. This affected 1 of 3
audit clients (#5). The finding is:

Review on 2/17/23 of the facilities abuse
investigation initiated on 2/7/23 revealed client #5
accused Staff A of giving him spankings. Staff A
had worked with client #5 on 2/4/23 to 2/5/23. All
staff were interviewed by the House Manager
(HM) and denied any abuse of clients. Staff A
denied spanking any client but did acknowledged
that he playfully shadow boxed with clients, but
never made physical contact. Client #2 revealed
she heard a loud clapping sound come from
client #5's room when Staff A was present, but did
not observe the client being struck. The facility
suspended Staff A and unsubstantiated abuse,
but did not file a report.

Interview on 2/16/23 with the HM revealed on
2/7/23 she was notified by Department of Social
Services (DSS) that they were conducting an
abuse investigation at the home on 2/7/23 due to
a report of physical abuse over the weekend
