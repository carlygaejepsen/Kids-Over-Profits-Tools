w0249 PROGRAM IMPLEMENTATION 'w0249

CFR(s): 483.440(d)(1)

As soon as the interdisciplinary team has formulated
a client's individual program plan, each client must
receive a continuous active treatment program
consisting of needed interventions and services in
sufficient number and frequency to support the
achievement of the objectives identified in the
individual program plan.

This STANDARD is NOT MET as evidenced by:

Based on observations, record reviews and
interviews, the facility failed to ensure 2 of 4 audit
clients (#2 and #5) received a continuous active
treatment program consisting of needed
interventions and services as identified in the
Individual Program Plans (IPP's). The findings are:

Observations throughout the survey on 7/20/26 and
7/21/26, clients #2 and #5 were observed sitting in
the living room/den for the majority of the
observations. During the observations, staff were
observed to be moving around the home and
instructing other clients to clean and assist with
meals. Clients #2 and #5 were not prompted or
encouraged to actively participate in any form of
active treatment.

Record review on 7/20/26 of client #2’s
Community/Home Life Assessment dated 1/21/26
revealed client #2 can prepare meals with physical
assistance, set the table independently and complete
household chores with verbal cues.

Observations on 7/21/26 and 7/21/26 revealed at no
time was client #2 encouraged to participate in any
meal preparation or household chores.

B. Record review on 7/20/26 of client #5's
Community/Home Life Assessment dated 1/21/26
revealed client #5 can set and clean the table with
verbal cues, complete household chores with
physical assistance. At no time was client #5
encouraged to participate in any meal preparation or
household chores. Interview on 7/21/26 with staff C
revealed clients #5 and #2 usually refuse to
participate in any chore or activity.

Interview on 7/21/26 with the site supervisor
confirmed all clients should participate in all
household chores and activities.

least by the qualified intellectual disability
professional and revised as necessary, including, but
not limited to situations in which the client has

identified in the individual program plan.

not continued after it was completed or revised. This
affected 2 of 4 audit clients (#2 and #5). The findings
are:

was observed putting a puzzle together. Further
observation throughout the survey revealed client #5
walking from room to room, sitting on different
chairs.

plan (IPP) dated 3/20/26, revealed a continuation of
the goals for client #5 included, handwashing, oral
hygiene, laundry and medication. Further review of
progress notes revealed client #5 had 100%
completion for all goals from November 2025
through June 2026.

Interview on 7/21/26 with the qualified intellectual
disabilities professional (QIDP), acknowledged the
goals had been revised and the criteria had
changed, the QIDP was unable to show
documentation of the revised goal.

B. During observations in the home on 7/20/26 at
4:00pm, client #2 was observed sitting at the dining
room table with a matching activity in front of him.
Throughout the remainder of observations on
7/20/26 through 7/21/26 client #2 sat in the living
room except when he went to the table to eat.

Review on 7/20/26 of client #2’s individual program
plan (IPP) dated 2/13/26, revealed goals for client #2
included handwashing, oral hygiene and laundry.
Further review of progress notes revealed client #2
had 100% completion for his laundry goal since
January 2025, 100% for handwashing goal since
December 2025 and 100% for oral hygiene goal since
July of 2025.

other members of the interdisciplinary team,
appropriate protective and preventive health
measures that include, but are not limited to training
clients and staff as needed in appropriate health and
hygiene methods.

implement appropriate health and hygiene methods.

#4's nails and a log book is kept in the home for

should be documented as well.
