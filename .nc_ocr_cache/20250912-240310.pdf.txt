form that includes a required HCPR
notification step and management
approval checklist.

HCPR reporting requirements into annual
mandatory training for all employees.

electronic reminders to notify the
Compliance Officer and Administrator of
any unresolved incidents requiring HCPR
reporting.

Oversight Committee: Monthly meetings
by the Quality Assurance (QA) Committee
to review all incidents and verify that
proper notifications were made.

incident reports for required notifications.
