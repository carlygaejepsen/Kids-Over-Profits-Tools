BlueWest Opportunities — Montford Home
Plan of Correction
Re: Survey Completed August 5, 2025

Please find below our facility’s plan of correction in response to your visit on
August 4&5, 2025. Our plan of correction addresses the E015 and W 130 citations.

£015. SUBSISTENCE NEEDS FOR STAFF AND PATIENTS,

Facilities must develop and implement emergency preparedness policies and
procedures, based on the emergency plan, risk assessment, and the communication
plan. The policies and procedures must be reviewed and updated every 2 years
[annually for LTC facilities]. At a minimum, the policies and procedures must address
the following: (1) The provision of subsistence needs for staff and Patients whether
they evacuate or shelter in place, include, but are not limited to the following: (i)
Food, water, medical and pharmaceutical supplies (ii) Alternate sources of energy to
maintain the following: (A) Temperatures to protect patient health and Safety and for
the safe and sanitary Storage of provisions. (B) Emergency lighting. (C) Fire detection,
extinguishing, and alarm systems. (D) Sewage and waste disposal.

The emergency supply foods which were found to have expired have been replaced. The
inventory has been reassessed, and all food items are within expiry.

The existing inventory sheet posted with the emergency supply will be updated to include
the expiration date for each item.

The emergency supply will be rechecked quarterly by the Dietary Manager to ensure
adequate provisions are maintained according to the policy.

Responsible Person(s): Dietary Manager
Mechanism to ensure compliance: Routine assessment and review.

Frequency of Mechanism: Quarterly

W 130 PROTECTION OF CLIENTS RIGHTS.

The facility must ensure the rights of all clients. Therefore, the facility must ensure
privacy during treatment and care of personal needs.

A training goal will be implemented by the QIDP to address the privacy needs of the client
relative to this survey.

Staff will be re-trained on client rights and the need to ensure privacy during treatment and
care of personal needs.

Responsible Person(s): QIDP

Mechanism to ensure compliance: Face-to-face staft training, implementation of formal
training goal, followed by routine assessment and review.

Frequency of Mechanism: Ongoing monitoring and assessment of client’s performance in
new training goal with monthly review of data. Immediate staff training followed by training
of new employees and review as needed.
