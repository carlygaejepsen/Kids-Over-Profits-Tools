Corrective Action:

The following measures will be put in place to correct errors in the Medication Administration Record
(MAR) :

1) The procedure for administration and recording of medication will be reviewed by the leadership

committee by October 31, 2024.
2) All staff involved in the administration of medication will be retrained by the Nurse on the proper
procedures in maintaining the MAR by November 25, 2024.

Prevention:

The following measures will be put in place to prevent errors in the MAR from occurring again:

1) The Program Director will be notified by the Nursing Assistant when a staff member is incomplete in
their documentation (beginning October 21, 2024).

2) The Program Director will provide supervision within 24 hours for staff to sort out any problems or
circumstances which may lead to MAR errors in the future

Monitoring:

1) The Nursing Assistant will check the MAR daily (beginning October 21, 2024) and provide the results
to the leadership committee on a monthly basis,

2) The frequency of this monitoring may be reduced after a criterion of 100% compliance for 10 days in a
row is met, At that time the monitoring frequency can be reduced to once per week.
