10A NCAC 130 .0102 INVESTIGATING AND
REPORTING HEALTH CARE PERSONNEL

The reporting by health care facilities to the
Department of all allegations against health care
personnel as defined in G.S. 131E-256 (a)(1),
including injuries of unknown source, shall be
done within 24 hours of the health care facility
becoming aware of the allegation. The results of
the health care facility's investigation shall be
submitted to the Department in accordance with
G.S. 131E-256(g).
